## Introduction

Welcome to Signal Pipe ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application Signal Pipe (the "App") handles information when you use our services.

Signal Pipe is a puzzle game where you rotate pipe segments to connect power sources to endpoints and complete circuits. We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly what data our App accesses and why.

---

## Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we **do not** do:

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or location.
- **No User Accounts:** The App does not require registration or login. There are no user accounts.
- **No Cloud Storage:** Your game progress, statistics, achievements, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.
- **No Data Selling:** We never sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use any analytics services to track your behavior.
- **No Advertising:** The App contains no advertisements of any kind.

---

## Information the App Stores Locally

To provide its functionality, Signal Pipe stores the following data locally on your device:

### 1. Game Progress Data
- **What:** Highest level reached, completed levels, star ratings, best move counts per level
- **Why:** To track your progress and allow you to continue where you left off
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 2. Statistics Data
- **What:** Total levels completed, total moves, total stars earned, total time played
- **Why:** To display your gameplay statistics
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 3. Streak Data
- **What:** Current play streak, best play streak, last played date
- **Why:** To maintain your daily streak and provide motivation
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 4. Achievement Data
- **What:** Unlocked achievements
- **Why:** To reward your accomplishments and track milestones
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 5. Daily Challenge Data
- **What:** Completed daily challenges, daily puzzle results
- **Why:** To track your daily challenge progress
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 6. Hints Data
- **What:** Available hints count
- **Why:** To manage your hint inventory across sessions
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 7. User Preferences
- **What:** Sound effects, music, vibration, energy animation, particles, and pure black mode settings
- **Why:** To remember your app settings
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 8. Tutorial Status
- **What:** Whether the tutorial has been completed
- **Why:** To avoid showing the tutorial to returning players
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

## How the App Works

1. You solve circuit puzzles by rotating pipe segments to connect power sources to endpoints
2. Your progress is saved locally after each completed level
3. Daily challenges are generated using a date-based algorithm (no server required)
4. **No data ever leaves your device** - everything happens locally

## Google Play In-App Updates

The App uses Google Play's In-App Update API to check for available updates. This feature:
- Communicates with Google Play Services on your device to check for newer versions of the App
- Does not transmit any personal data or gameplay information
- Is handled entirely by Google Play Services, subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
All your data is stored locally on your device in app-private storage:
- Game statistics and progress
- Achievements and milestones
- Streak data
- App preferences and settings

### No Cloud Sync
We do not offer cloud synchronization. Your data never leaves your device.

### Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Uninstalling the App removes all App-related data
- You can clear App data anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Signal Pipe > Clear Data
- You can reset progress from within the App via Settings > Reset Progress
- Your progress cannot be recovered once deleted

## Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data:

1. **Disable Sound Effects:** Toggle off in App settings
2. **Disable Background Music:** Toggle off in App settings
3. **Disable Vibration:** Toggle off in App settings
4. **Disable Energy Animation:** Toggle off in App settings
5. **Disable Particles:** Toggle off in App settings
6. **Toggle Pure Black Mode:** Toggle on/off in App settings
7. **Reset Progress:** Reset all game data from App settings
8. **Clear Data:** Clear all App data via Android Settings
9. **Uninstall:** Remove the App and all associated data at any time
