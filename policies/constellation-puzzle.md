## Introduction

Welcome to Constellation ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application Constellation (the "App") handles information when you use our services.

Constellation is a puzzle game where you draw constellations by connecting stars in the correct order. We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly what data our App accesses and why.

---

## Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we **do not** do:

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or location.
- **No User Accounts:** The App does not require registration or login. There are no user accounts.
- **No Cloud Storage:** Your game progress, statistics, achievements, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.
- **No Data Selling:** We never sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use any analytics services to track your behavior.
- **No Internet Access:** The App does not require or use internet connectivity. It works completely offline.
- **No Advertising:** The App contains no advertisements of any kind.

---

## Information the App Stores Locally

To provide its functionality, Constellation stores the following data locally on your device:

### 1. Game Progress Data
- **What:** Highest level reached, completed levels, total stars visited, current level progress
- **Why:** To track your progress and allow you to continue where you left off
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 2. Streak Data
- **What:** Current winning streak, last played date
- **Why:** To maintain your streak and provide motivation
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 3. Achievement Data
- **What:** Unlocked achievements, progress toward achievements
- **Why:** To reward your accomplishments and track milestones
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 4. Daily Challenge Data
- **What:** Completed daily challenges count
- **Why:** To track your daily challenge progress and award hints
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 5. Hints Data
- **What:** Available hints count (starts at 3, maximum 10)
- **Why:** To manage your hint inventory across sessions
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 6. User Preferences
- **What:** Sound effects, music, vibration, and particle effects settings
- **Why:** To remember your app settings
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 7. Tutorial Status
- **What:** Whether the tutorial has been completed
- **Why:** To avoid showing the tutorial to returning players
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

## How the App Works

1. You solve constellation puzzles by connecting stars in the correct order
2. Your progress is saved locally after each completed level
3. Daily challenges are generated using a date-based algorithm (no server required)
4. **No data ever leaves your device** - everything happens locally

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
All your data is stored locally on your device in app-private storage:
- Game statistics and progress
- Achievements and milestones
- Streak data
- App preferences and settings

### No Cloud Sync
We do not offer cloud synchronization. Your data never leaves your device.

### Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Uninstalling the App removes all App-related data
- You can clear App data anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Constellation > Clear Data
- Your progress cannot be recovered once deleted

## Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data:

1. **Disable Sound Effects:** Toggle off in App settings
2. **Disable Background Music:** Toggle off in App settings
3. **Disable Vibration:** Toggle off in App settings
4. **Disable Particle Effects:** Toggle off in App settings
5. **Clear Data:** Clear all App data via Android Settings
6. **Uninstall:** Remove the App and all associated data at any time
