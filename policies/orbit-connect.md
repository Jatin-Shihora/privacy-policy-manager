## Introduction

Welcome to Orbit Connect ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application Orbit Connect (the "App") handles information when you use our services.

Orbit Connect is a puzzle game where you rotate concentric orbital rings to align glowing nodes and form connections. We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly what data our App accesses and why.

---

## Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we **do not** do:

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or location.
- **No User Accounts:** The App does not require registration or login. There are no user accounts.
- **No Cloud Storage:** Your game progress, statistics, achievements, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.
- **No Data Selling:** We never sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use any analytics services to track your behavior.
- **No Advertising:** The App contains no advertisements of any kind.
- **No Third-Party Tracking:** We do not integrate any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

---

## Information the App Stores Locally

To provide its functionality, Orbit Connect stores the following data locally on your device:

### 1. Game Progress Data
- **What:** Highest unlocked level, completed levels, stars earned per level, best moves per level, attempt counts
- **Why:** To track your progress across 200+ levels and allow you to continue where you left off
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 2. Player Statistics
- **What:** Total levels completed, total moves made, total play time, total connections, hints used, perfect levels count
- **Why:** To display your cumulative gameplay statistics
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 3. Streak Data
- **What:** Current play streak, longest streak achieved, last played date
- **Why:** To maintain your daily play streak and track your longest streak
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 4. Daily Challenge Data
- **What:** Daily challenge completion status, moves used on daily challenge
- **Why:** To track your daily challenge progress (generated locally using a date-based algorithm — no server required)
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 5. Hint Data
- **What:** Available hints count (maximum 5), last hint regeneration timestamp
- **Why:** To manage your hint inventory across sessions (hints regenerate at 1 per 4 hours)
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 6. Achievement Data
- **What:** Unlocked achievements and their unlock dates (12 achievements total)
- **Why:** To reward your accomplishments and track milestones
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 7. User Preferences
- **What:** Sound effects, music, vibration, particle effects, pure black mode, and colorblind mode settings
- **Why:** To remember your app settings
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

### 8. Tutorial Status
- **What:** Whether the tutorial and linked-orbits tutorial have been completed
- **Why:** To avoid showing tutorials to returning players
- **Storage:** Local DataStore preferences on your device only

## How the App Works

1. You solve orbital puzzles by rotating concentric rings to align nodes across adjacent orbits
2. Your progress is saved locally after each completed level
3. Daily challenges are generated using a date-based deterministic algorithm (no server required)
4. Levels are procedurally generated using deterministic seeds — the same level number always produces the same puzzle
5. **No gameplay data ever leaves your device** — everything happens locally

## Network Usage

The App is primarily an offline experience and does not require internet connectivity to function. However, the App uses one network feature:

### Google Play In-App Updates
- **What:** The App checks the Google Play Store for available app updates on launch
- **Why:** To ensure you have the latest version with bug fixes and improvements
- **Data Sent:** Only standard Google Play update check requests (no personal or gameplay data is transmitted)
- **Failure Behavior:** If the check fails (e.g., no internet, Play Services unavailable), the App continues normally without interruption
- **Control:** This is handled entirely by the Google Play system and is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
All your data is stored locally on your device in app-private storage:
- Game progress and level completions
- Player statistics and play time
- Achievements and milestones
- Streak data
- App preferences and settings

### No Cloud Sync
We do not offer cloud synchronization. Your gameplay data never leaves your device.

### Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- **Uninstall:** Uninstalling the App removes all App-related data
- **Clear Data:** You can clear App data anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Orbit Connect > Clear Data
- **Reset Progress:** You can reset all progress from within the App via Settings > Reset Progress
- Your progress cannot be recovered once deleted
