## Introduction

Welcome to Light Path ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application Light Path (the "App") handles information when you use our services.

Light Path is a laser reflection puzzle game where you rotate mirrors to guide a beam of light from a source to a target. We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly what data our App accesses and why.

---

## Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we **do not** do:

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or location.
- **No User Accounts:** The App does not require registration or login. There are no user accounts.
- **No Cloud Storage:** Your game progress, statistics, achievements, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.
- **No Data Selling:** We never sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use any analytics services to track your behavior.
- **No Advertising:** The App contains no advertisements of any kind.
- **No Internet Usage:** The App does not use the internet in any way. It is fully offline.

---

## Information the App Stores Locally

To provide its functionality, Light Path stores the following data locally on your device:

### 1. Game Progress Data
- **What:** Completed levels, star ratings, move counts, completion times, highest level reached
- **Why:** To track your progress and allow you to continue where you left off
- **Storage:** Local SharedPreferences on your device only

### 2. Statistics Data
- **What:** Total levels completed, total moves made, total play time, best completion times
- **Why:** To display your gameplay statistics
- **Storage:** Local SharedPreferences on your device only

### 3. Streak Data
- **What:** Current play streak, last played date
- **Why:** To maintain your daily streak and provide motivation
- **Storage:** Local SharedPreferences on your device only

### 4. Achievement Data
- **What:** Unlocked achievements, progress toward achievements
- **Why:** To reward your accomplishments and track milestones
- **Storage:** Local SharedPreferences on your device only

### 5. Daily Challenge Data
- **What:** Completed daily challenges and their results
- **Why:** To track your daily challenge progress
- **Storage:** Local SharedPreferences on your device only

### 6. User Preferences
- **What:** Sound effects and music toggle settings
- **Why:** To remember your app settings
- **Storage:** Local SharedPreferences on your device only

## How the App Works

1. You solve light beam puzzles by rotating mirrors to guide a laser from a source to a target
2. Your progress is saved locally after each completed level
3. Daily challenges are generated using a date-based algorithm (no server required)
4. Levels are generated using a deterministic algorithm based on the level number (no server required)
5. **No data ever leaves your device** — all game data is stored and processed locally

---

## Internet Usage

**The App does not use the internet.** Light Path is a fully offline application. It does not request the `INTERNET` permission and does not communicate with any servers.

---

## Permissions Explained

**Light Path requests no special permissions.**

The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, internet, or any other sensitive permissions. It operates entirely within its own local storage space.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
All your data is stored locally on your device in app-private storage:
- Game statistics and progress
- Achievements and milestones
- Streak data
- App preferences and settings

### No Cloud Sync
We do not offer cloud synchronization. Your game data never leaves your device.

### Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Uninstalling the App removes all App-related data
- You can clear App data anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Light Path > Clear Data
- You can reset progress from within the App via Settings > Reset Progress
- Your progress cannot be recovered once deleted
