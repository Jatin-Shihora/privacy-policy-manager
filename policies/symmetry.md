## Introduction

Welcome to Symmetry ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application Symmetry (the "App") handles information when you use our services.

Symmetry is a minimalist mirror pattern puzzle game where you recreate symmetric patterns on a grid. We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly what data our App accesses and why.

---

## Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we **do not** do:

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or location.
- **No User Accounts:** The App does not require registration or login. There are no user accounts.
- **No Cloud Storage:** Your game progress, statistics, achievements, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.
- **No Data Selling:** We never sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use any analytics services to track your behavior.
- **No Advertising:** The App contains no advertisements of any kind.

---

## Information the App Stores Locally

To provide its functionality, Symmetry stores the following data locally on your device:

### 1. Game Progress Data
- **What:** Highest level reached, completed levels, star ratings, best move counts, times played per level
- **Why:** To track your progress and allow you to continue where you left off
- **Storage:** Local Room database and DataStore on your device only

### 2. Statistics Data
- **What:** Total moves, total play time
- **Why:** To display your gameplay statistics
- **Storage:** Local DataStore on your device only

### 3. Streak Data
- **What:** Current play streak, best streak, last played date
- **Why:** To maintain your daily streak and provide motivation
- **Storage:** Local DataStore on your device only

### 4. Achievement Data
- **What:** Unlocked achievements and their unlock dates
- **Why:** To reward your accomplishments and track milestones
- **Storage:** Local Room database on your device only

### 5. Daily Challenge Data
- **What:** Completed daily challenges and their results
- **Why:** To track your daily challenge progress
- **Storage:** Local Room database on your device only

### 6. Hint Data
- **What:** Remaining hint count, last daily hint bonus date
- **Why:** To manage hint availability and daily login rewards
- **Storage:** Local DataStore on your device only

### 7. User Preferences
- **What:** Sound effects, background music, vibration, particle effects, and pure black mode settings
- **Why:** To remember your app settings
- **Storage:** Local DataStore on your device only

## How the App Works

1. You solve symmetry puzzles by tapping cells to recreate mirror patterns across vertical, horizontal, diagonal, or rotational axes
2. Your progress is saved locally after each completed level
3. Daily challenges are generated using a date-based algorithm (no server required)
4. All 1000+ levels are generated using a deterministic algorithm based on the level number (no server required)
5. Hints are awarded through daily login bonuses and level milestones
6. **No personal data ever leaves your device** - game data is stored and processed locally

---

## Internet Usage

The App uses a minimal internet connection solely for the following purpose:

| Feature | Purpose | Data Sent |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| In-App Update Check | To check if a newer version is available on Google Play Store | No personal data - only standard Play Store update check |

**Note:** The in-app update check is handled entirely by the Google Play Core library. We do not operate any servers and do not receive any data from this process. If no internet connection is available, the App continues to function normally without updates.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
All your data is stored locally on your device in app-private storage:
- Game progress and level completions
- Achievements and milestones
- Streak data
- Hint counts
- App preferences and settings

### No Cloud Sync
We do not offer cloud synchronization. Your game data never leaves your device.

### Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Uninstalling the App removes all App-related data
- You can clear App data anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Symmetry > Clear Data
- You can reset progress from within the App via Settings > Reset Progress
- Your progress cannot be recovered once deleted
