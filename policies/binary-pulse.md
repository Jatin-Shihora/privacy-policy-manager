## Introduction

Welcome to Binary Pulse ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application Binary Pulse (the "App") handles information when you use our services.

Binary Pulse is a Lights Out-style puzzle game where you toggle nodes on a grid to reach a target state. We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly what data our App accesses and why.

---

## Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we **do not** do:

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or location.
- **No User Accounts:** The App does not require registration or login. There are no user accounts.
- **No Cloud Storage:** Your game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device only.
- **No Data Selling:** We never sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use any analytics services to track your behavior.
- **No Advertising:** The App contains no advertisements of any kind.
- **No Internet Usage:** The App does not use any internet connection. It is fully offline.

---

## Information the App Stores Locally

To provide its functionality, Binary Pulse stores the following data locally on your device:

### 1. Game Progress Data
- **What:** Highest level unlocked, best move counts for each completed level
- **Why:** To track your progress and allow you to continue where you left off
- **Storage:** Local SharedPreferences on your device only

### 2. User Preferences
- **What:** Sound effects enabled/disabled, background music enabled/disabled
- **Why:** To remember your audio settings between sessions
- **Storage:** Local SharedPreferences on your device only

**That's it.** The App stores no other data. All 1000 levels are generated using a deterministic algorithm based on the level number - no downloads or server communication is required.

---

## How the App Works

1. You solve puzzles by tapping nodes on a grid to toggle them and their neighbors
2. Your progress (highest level unlocked and best move counts) is saved locally after each completed level
3. Levels are generated using a deterministic reverse-construction algorithm based on the level number (no server required)
4. Sound effects and background music play locally from bundled audio files
5. **No data ever leaves your device** - all game data is stored and processed locally

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
All your data is stored locally on your device in app-private storage:
- Game progress (highest level unlocked, best move counts)
- Audio preferences (sound effects and music toggles)

### No Cloud Sync
We do not offer cloud synchronization. Your game data never leaves your device.

### Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Uninstalling the App removes all App-related data
- You can clear App data anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Binary Pulse > Clear Data
- Your progress cannot be recovered once deleted
